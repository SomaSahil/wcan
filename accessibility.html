Accessibility

We want everyone in our community to be able to use this website.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Our commitment

We aim for this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA.

What we have done

  • Clear headings and simple layouts that work on phones and computers.
  • Text with strong colour contrast and easy-to-read fonts.
  • Full keyboard use, a skip link and visible focus.
  • Descriptions for images and labelled form fields.

Known issues

We are not aware of any major issues right now. The community survey is run on Microsoft Forms, so it follows Microsoft’s accessibility features rather than ours. If you have trouble with it, contact us and we will help.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, or you need information in another format, email wcangroup[at]outlook[dot]com. We will do our best to help.
