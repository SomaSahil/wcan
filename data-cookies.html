Data and Cookies

What data this website uses, and what it doesn’t.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Cookies

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The contact form sets one short-lived cookie that helps block spam. It is deleted when you close your browser.

Analytics

We do not currently use website analytics. If we add them, we will update this page.

Third-party services

To load fonts and page features, this site uses Google Fonts and the jsDelivr content network. When you visit, these services may receive your IP address and browser details. Our community survey is hosted on Microsoft Forms, which has its own privacy practices.

Contact form data

Messages you send are emailed to us. We do not store them in a database on this website.

Where data is stored

Messages arrive in our organisation’s email inbox. Survey responses are stored in Microsoft Forms. Our email and survey providers may store data in Australia or overseas, depending on how their systems work.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To ask about your data, see our Privacy Policy or email wcangroup[at]outlook[dot]com.
