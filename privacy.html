Privacy Policy

How we look after your personal information, in plain English.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

Whittlesea Climate Action Network (WCAN) is a community climate action network in Victoria, Australia. In this policy, “we” means WCAN. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the topic and your message.
  • Community survey: whatever you enter in the survey. It is run on Microsoft Forms.

Our web host may also keep basic technical records, such as your IP address and the pages visited, to keep the site running and secure.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to you, to understand what the community thinks, and to plan our work. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who sees it

Our volunteers who need it for the task. We also use services to run this site, such as our web host, email and Microsoft Forms. We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and survey responses only as long as we need them for the purpose you gave them. Contact messages are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Survey responses are kept while we review the results and develop our Community Climate Action Plan, then deleted or de-identified.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or email is completely secure. Please don’t send sensitive information through the contact form.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact
