What we’re working on

Our current areas of focus include:

Community Conversations

Understanding what climate resilience means to our community is at the heart of our work. Through focus groups, informal meet-ups, resilience mapping and community listening, we’re building strong relationships with people across the City of Whittlesea.

These conversations help us understand local experiences, priorities and strengths. They also remind us that many people share our mission and commitment to caring for the environment.

Community Engagement

We connect with the community through creative, engaging activities that encourage conversation and learning around eco-friendliness.

Our Waste Quiz uses fun, educational questions to start unlikely conversations about waste and sustainability. Our Climate Pledge Tree invites people to make a personal commitment to caring for nature by writing their pledge on a leaf and adding it to the tree, blossoming it into a hopeful beacon of eager community messages and commitments to climate action.

Event Participation

Local events are an important way for us to raise awareness, share information and connect with the wider community.

We regularly participate in events including Family Fun Days, Waste Safari, the WCAN Festival, the Melbourne Polytechnic Expo, Sustainathon and the Save the Soil campaign. Through these events, we’ve engaged with more than 500 people, including many newly arrived and multicultural City of Whittlesea community members.

Responding to Local Concerns

Listening and responding to community concerns is an important part of our work.

Our Community Voices on Climate survey helps us understand the personal experiences of climate change and extreme weather, as well as the ideas and priorities of people living in the City of Whittlesea.

We also support practical action to community concerns by participating in regular clean-up days and planting activities with local groups and volunteers.

Collaborating with Local Groups Organisations

Collaboration strengthens our impact and helps build a more informed and resilient community.

We work with local groups, organisations and initiatives to share knowledge and support collective climate action. Our partners include Thomastown Neighbourhood House, Climate Action Network Australia, Friends of the Earth, Leaders of Tomorrow, Friends of Edgars Creek, the CFA, Lalor Landcare, DisasterWise Communities, Melbourne Arboretum and the City of Whittlesea.

These partnerships provide us with regular opportunities for training, collaboration, community connection and shared learning.

Laying the Foundation for a Future Community Climate Action Plan

As we continue listening, learning and working with our community, we’re laying the foundations for a Community Climate Action Plan that reflects the voices, needs and priorities of people across the City of Whittlesea.

With the support of our team, volunteers, partners and community members, we aim to help create a City of Whittlesea that is informed, prepared and connected. Together, we can strengthen our community’s resilience, reduce the impacts of climate change, and help protect the health of our environment for current and future generations.
